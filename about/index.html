About Greendominium

Brady Ewing is gathering the notes, stills, and questions that will shape a house inside a greenhouse of his own. This site is the public scrapbook.

Not a product line

Greendominium is a name for an idea: a livable core under glass, the barndominium of a greenhouse. The builds in the gallery were designed by other architects and made by other greenhouse shops. Each page names them.

We show those homes because they already work. They are credited inspiration, not Greendominium inventory. Architect, greenhouse maker, other builders, and photographers are named on every profile — with links to their own sites when we have them.

How the catalog stays small

Brady has almost no spare time. CoS maintains this site. A new example is a markdown file plus images — same fields every time, so the gallery stays scannable.
