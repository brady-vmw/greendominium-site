A private home and office in Rekkem: a timber-frame box inside a steel-and-glass greenhouse. Client and concept by KASECO+ / Vandewalle-Wielfaert; the build is often shown as a Norhage Industri / Deforche product reference.

Location
Rekkem, Belgium
Climate
Temperate maritime; glass shell for microclimate + wind shelter more than deep freeze
Greenhouse
30 m × 12.20 m (~98 × 40 ft) · ridge height 9 m (~30 ft) · footprint ~366 m²
House inside
~170 m² (~1,830 ft²) timber-frame “box”
Status
Built private home / office · often shown as Norhage Industri / Deforche product reference

Unique features

  • Clear box-in-box / double-skin: steel-and-glass outer shell, timber house + garden + office inside
  • Single glass walls, laminated glass roof; fully screwed so the greenhouse can be dismantled
  • Designed toward off-grid (water / sewer / power still evolving)
  • Productized story via Norhage Industri + Deforche Construction Group
