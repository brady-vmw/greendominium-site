Ulland, Kongsberg, Norway

Greenhouse Home — Kongsberg

A red timber barn-house under a 38-foot Danish glass shell in the spruce forest.

Credits

The people who designed and made this home.

Architect / designer
Margit-Kristine Solibakke Klev (Outline Arkitektur)
Greenhouse / shell
Drivadan (Denmark)
Photos
Nadia Norskott · Richard Powers / Inside Living (Guardian)

A family home in the spruce forest just outside Kongsberg, with a stream and waterfall nearby. The red timber core sits under a tall Drivadan greenhouse so the space between house and glass can be lived in most of the year.

Location
Ulland, Kongsberg, Norway
Climate
Harsh Nordic winters; glass shell creates a usable “in-between” season most of the year
Greenhouse
~360–380 m² (~3,900–4,100 ft²); ~11.6 m / 38 ft tall (Drivadan standard model)
House inside
~250 m² (~2,700 ft²) living area · 2 stories · red-painted timber (barn-inspired)
Status
Completed ~2019 · family home (architect-owner)

Unique features

  • Classic “house in a house”: red wooden core wrapped on two sides by glass; north/east glass meets the roof eaves
  • Kitchen opens to the greenhouse via a 4 m × 2.5 m folding glass wall — indoor/outdoor becomes one room in summer
  • Rooftop / upper outdoor kitchen under glass for gatherings
  • Fruit trees and veg under glass (figs, grapes, citrus, cherries, plums) against snow outside
  • Auto roof vents above ~68°F; openings on three sides for summer cross-ventilation
  • Rooms against the rear greenhouse wall so windows can open to true outdoors / waterfall sound
