The idea

What a Greendominium is

A house inside a greenhouse. The glass keeps wind and weather off the living core. In a cold climate that means a usable in-between season. In a milder one, it means shelter, a microclimate, and a garden that does not blow away.

01

Glass shell

A commercial or custom greenhouse wraps the lot — tall enough for a house, trees, and weather that stays outside.

02

Livable core

A timber or barn-like house sits inside: bedrooms, kitchen, office. Windows can open to the garden or, in some plans, to true outdoors.

03

In-between season

The gap between house and glass is the point — fruit trees, a folding wall to the kitchen, a place to sit when snow is on the ground.
